This is **pre-existing from Phase 6** — NOT introduced by v1.1. Carry as v1.2 polish candidate (refactor the badge to `<span role="button">` with keyboard handling, or restructure NavButton).

### 3. CSV round-trip apostrophe asymmetry (by design)
Phase-9 TB CSV apostrophe-prefixes leading-zero codes for Excel text-affinity (`'0410` instead of `0410`). Phase-7 ImportTB does not strip the apostrophe on re-import. A round-trip (export → re-import without opening in Excel first) would produce account codes like `'0410` in the raw CSV string. **By design** — no v1.1 requirement specifies round-trip guarantee. Document as scope boundary if round-trip becomes a v1.2+ requirement.
